## Chosen transfer: current-status review routes

All six review records share one launch date, making the timeline mostly empty. Replace it with one cohort origin branching into four directly labelled status destinations and exact counts. One selectable token represents one submission. Thin, equal-width curves encode grouping; they imply neither traffic volume nor historical progression.

Selection connects the token to a stable inspector with title, product, status, revision, launch date, separate findings/unknowns and Open review. Status buttons filter the existing queue. No invented history, moving traffic, risk score or implied approval from zero findings. Preserve the shell and other pages. The distinctive shape serves the references' object-to-record correspondence.
